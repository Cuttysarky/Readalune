# Welcome to Readalune

## A Room of Your Own

Readalune is a quiet place to read. Bring your own text files, Markdown notes, web pages saved as HTML, DRM-free EPUB books and PDFs, and Readalune turns them into calm, comfortable pages that you turn one at a time, just like a printed book.

Nothing you read ever leaves this computer. There are no accounts, no store, no tracking and no network connections. Your library is simply a folder on your disk, and you can back it up by copying it.

This short guide is itself a book in your library. Use it to try things out: turn pages, change the font, make a highlight or two. When you are done you can remove it from the library like any other book.

## Turning Pages

Press the **right arrow**, **Page Down** or the **space bar** to go forward, and the **left arrow** or **Page Up** to go back. You can also scroll with your mouse wheel or trackpad, swipe on a touch screen, or click the edges of the page.

At the bottom of the screen you will always see how far along you are. The number on the right is the percentage of the book you have read. On the left, Readalune shows your page number. *Click it* to switch between:

- the page you are on and the total number of pages,
- your position in the book, which never changes when you resize the text,
- the time left in the current chapter,
- the time left in the whole book.

Time estimates start with an average reading speed and then quietly learn your own pace as you turn pages.

The thin line in the middle is a map of the whole book. Each small notch marks a chapter. Hover over it to see where a spot is, and click to jump there. When you jump, a **Back to page** button appears so you can return to where you were.

## Making It Yours

Click **Aa** at the top of the page (or press **A**) to open the reading settings. You can choose from six page themes, from bright *Day* to the true black of *Midnight*. You can pick a font, make the text larger or smaller, and adjust line spacing and margins.

You can also choose between justified and left-aligned text, indented or spaced paragraphs, and one or two pages side by side. On a wide window, two pages appear automatically, like an open book.

The **Brightness** slider dims the page, and **Warm light** gives it an amber tint that is easier on the eyes at night.

> Tip: press **+** and **−** to change the text size at any time, and **F** for full screen.

## Highlights, Notes and Bookmarks

Select any text with your mouse and a small menu appears. Choose one of five colours to highlight the passage, or add a note to it. From the same menu you can copy the text, save a word to your Word Journal, search the book for it, or have it read aloud.

Click a highlight later to change its colour, edit its note, or remove it.

To bookmark a page, press **B** or click the ribbon button at the top. A ribbon appears in the corner of every bookmarked page.

Press **N** to see all your highlights, notes and bookmarks for the book you are reading. From there you can jump to any of them, or export everything as a tidy Markdown file. The **Notes** tab in the library collects highlights from all your books in one place.

## Finding Your Way

Press **T** to open the table of contents. Readalune finds chapters automatically: it reads the outline of PDFs and EPUBs, recognises Markdown and HTML headings, and spots lines such as *Chapter One* or *Part II* in plain text.

Press **Ctrl+F** or **/** to search the whole book. Each result shows the chapter and the page it is on. Press **G** to go straight to a page, a position or a percentage.

Use the square brackets, **[** and **]**, to move to the previous or next chapter.

## Your Word Journal and Reading Aloud

When you select a word and choose **Save word**, Readalune keeps it in your **Word Journal**, together with the sentence you found it in. The library's Word Journal tab lists every word you have collected; mark words as learned, or export them as a spreadsheet file.

Press **R** to have the book read aloud from the current page, using the voices built into your operating system. The sentence being read is highlighted, and pages turn by themselves. You can change the speed and the voice from the small player that appears.

## Your Library

The library shows all of your books with their covers and progress. Books from EPUB and PDF files keep their cover image; everything else gets a simple typographic cover of its own.

Use the filters to see books you are reading, have not started, or have finished. Sort by title, author, progress or when you last read them. Group books into **collections** from each book's menu, and edit a book's title and author in its details.

The **Reading Stats** tab shows how much you have read today and this week, your current reading streak, the books you have finished and your reading speed. These numbers are kept only on this computer.

## A Note on Your Books

Readalune is free, open-source software, provided as it is, without warranty. Apart from the classics on the Classics tab, it does not include, sell, download or share books, and it never removes DRM or other copy protection. Please import only files you have the right to read: your own writing, public-domain works, open-licensed books, or books you have obtained legitimately in a DRM-free format.

The books on the Classics tab come from Project Gutenberg and are in the public domain in the United States. Copyright rules differ in other countries, so if you live elsewhere, check before you read or share them. You will find the details under **Legal & licenses** in the About window.

That's everything. Close this guide with **Esc**, import a book with **Ctrl+O**, and enjoy your reading room.
